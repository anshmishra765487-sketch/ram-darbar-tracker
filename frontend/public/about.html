Ram Darbar Tracker is a free transport management app for truck owners, transporters and goods consignment agents in India. Instead of registers and WhatsApp notes, keep every trip, bilty, truck, driver, kharcha and party payment in one place.

What you can do with Ram Darbar Tracker

Trips & bilty

Add a consignment with party, route, goods, truck, driver, sale amount and advance. Print a proper bilty PDF for the party at delivery time.

Kharcha tracking

Record fuel, toll, maintenance, driver advance and other expenses against a trip or a truck, so the real profit of each trip is visible.

Party payments

Track cash, bank, UPI and cheque payments received from each party, and see the pending amount per trip and per party.

Trucks & drivers

Keep registration number, model, capacity, driver phone, licence and salary details handy. Duplicate truck numbers are blocked automatically.

Reports

Outstanding aging (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), party ledger, truck-wise profit, driver payouts, monthly revenue vs expense chart and CSV export.

Works offline

The app runs in your browser and stores data on your own device, so it keeps working even when the network is weak on the highway.

Who is it for

  • Truck owners who run one truck or a small fleet
  • Transport contractors who book consignments for parties
  • Commission agents who need a clean bilty and payment record
  • Drivers who want to note down trip kharcha on the phone

Frequently asked questions

What is Ram Darbar Tracker?

Ram Darbar Tracker is a free truck transport management app. It keeps trip and bilty records, truck and driver details, kharcha (fuel, toll, maintenance), party payments, pending dues and profit reports in one place.

Is Ram Darbar Tracker free?

Yes. Ram Darbar Tracker is free to use and runs in the browser on mobile and desktop. It also works without internet because the data is stored on your own device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app saves everything on your phone or computer, so you can add trips and expenses even without network. Data can be synced across devices when cloud sync is enabled.

Can I print a bilty from the app?

Yes. Every trip can be exported as a printable bilty PDF showing party, route, truck, driver, sale, advance, expenses, pending amount and profit.

Start using it

No installation and no sign-up needed to try it. Open the app, add your first truck and trip, and the dashboard starts showing your revenue, expenses, pending amount and profit.
